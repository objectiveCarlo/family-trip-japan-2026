⚡ Decisions & Reminders

Most of the big bookings are done — this is what's left to watch, plus the arrival admin.

🌇
Shibuya Sky — Sunset Slot
Book ~Sep 18
Official tickets (shibuya-scramble-square.com) open on a rolling 14-day window — for the Oct 2 visit that's around Sep 18. No fixed release time, so check that morning with payment ready. Sunset slots (15:00+) go first and can sell out within hours. Walk-up draws from the same pool — sold out online means sold out at the door.
🍽️
Gonpachi Nishiazabu — Table for 9
Reserve ahead
The "Kill Bill" izakaya. Book the Nishi-Azabu branch (the three-storey atrium one) for the Day 7 dinner via Tabelog or the official site — a party of 9 needs advance notice, and after Shibuya Sky it'll be prime time.
💳
Suica / IC Cards
On arrival
Set up Suica (or PASMO) for everyone at Narita, or add one to each phone via Apple/Google Pay before landing. Works on every train and bus for the whole trip — Kamakura, the Enoden, and all of Tokyo — plus convenience stores.
🎢
PokéPark app — Day 6
Before you go
Tickets are booked for Oct 1. Some rides need a free limited-access ticket claimed in the official PokéPark app once you're through the gate — install it beforehand and make sure someone has mobile data. Save the entry QR offline.
🛂
Travel documents
✅ Visas done
Ate Lyn & Tita Ana's visas are secured. Everyone: carry printed copies of the visa page, return flight, Airbnb bookings, and this itinerary — Japan immigration sometimes asks.

🍜 Must-Eat List

Reliable picks for a group of 9 — mostly chains with real tables. Classic ramen counters only seat a handful, so those mean splitting up. Tap a name for the map.

Kura SushiSushi · chain

Conveyor-belt sushi, ~¥115 a plate, ordered from the seat-side touchscreen. Every few empty plates drops a capsule-toy prize — kid catnip. Big branches at Asakusa, Shibuya and Ikebukuro.

Map ↗
Uobei ShibuyaSushi · Shibuya

Genki Sushi's tablet-order bar — plates rocket down a rail straight to your seat. Cheap, fast and fun, right by the Day 7 Shibuya evening. Counter only, so the group spreads along the bar.

Map ↗
IppudoRamen · chain

The easy "not Ichiran" tonkotsu pick — proper tables, picture menus, branches everywhere (Ginza, Shinjuku, Roppongi, Ebisu). The one to choose when all 9 want ramen together.

Map ↗
AFURIRamen · light

Clear yuzu-salt ramen, much lighter than tonkotsu — good for the older palates. Harajuku (near Day 5), Ebisu and Shinjuku branches; some have tables, some are ticket-machine + counter.

Map ↗
TonchinRamen · Ikebukuro

Rich Tokyo-style tonkotsu a few minutes from the Pokémon Center. Has table seating and handles bigger groups better than most ramen-ya — a fit for the Day 5 area.

bills ShichirigahamaBrunch · Kamakura

The ricotta-hotcakes-with-an-ocean-view brunch spot, ~10 min from the Koshigoe house. A lazy Day 2 or Day 3 morning — book ahead on weekends, it fills up.

Build-your-own Japanese curry — pick the rice size, spice level and toppings. Everywhere, cheap, quick, and the safe bet for picky eaters.
