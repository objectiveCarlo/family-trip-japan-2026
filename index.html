⚡ Decisions & Deadlines

🏠
Kamakura Accommodation
July 1
Decide between Airbnb (whole house, more homey) vs hotel near Kamakura Station. Group of 10 with older guests — consider elevator access and proximity to train.
🗼
Tokyo Hotel Confirmation
July 1
Leaning toward The Kanzashi Tokyo Asakusa. Confirm rooms for 10 pax (6 nights, Sep 29–Oct 4). Call/email hotel directly to block rooms.
🛂
Visa — Ate Lyn & Tita Ana
Apply July
Both need to apply for Japan tourist visa in July. Prepare supporting docs: bank statements, ITR, flight & hotel bookings, employment certificate.
🎮
PokéPark KANTO Tickets
Jul 30 · 5pm PH
Tickets drop exactly 2 months before visit date (Sep 30). Release is July 30 at 6pm JST / 5pm PH time. Use the international booking site. Sells out in minutes — have card ready!
💳
Suica Cards & IC Cards
No deadline
Get Suica (or PASMO) IC cards for all 10 on arrival. Works on trains, buses, and convenience stores across Tokyo and Kamakura. Can load via phone (Apple/Google Pay) or at station kiosks.
